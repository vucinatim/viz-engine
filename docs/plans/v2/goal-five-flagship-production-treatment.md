## Treatment

A 48-second club performance begins as intimate human presence, grows into a projected signal architecture, fractures into abstraction, and returns as a collective full-stage peak and release.

Unlike Signal Cathedral this is not a single custom procedural world; unlike Afterlight Assembly it is not a single Stage layer. Existing reusable components form a human-led, multi-layer compositor score.

This document and its manifest are derived from one authored blueprint at `tools/foundation/author-goal-five-production-treatment.ts`. They own proposed direction only and add no hidden product semantics.

## Emotional and visual direction

The emotional arc is anticipation and intimacy → arrival and confidence → fracture and rebuild → suspended transformation → collective peak and release. The hierarchy is human Stage subject → signal architecture → atmosphere and impacts → 2D music horizon → bounded exposure punctuation.

Glossy black venue surfaces, emissive wire/signal structures, translucent particle haze, grounded authored models, and one thin graphic spectrum line. Amber/cyan human keying leads; magenta is impact contrast; internal Stage strobes and blinders remain disabled; dedicated white accents are exact and short.

Camera contract: Stage Scene alone owns the primary Panoramic Sweep at fixed 48-second duration. Light Tunnel, Morph Shapes, and Particle System retain independent cameras and are compositor-joined projections, never represented as one shared world or shared occlusion space.

Transition contract: Each exact P1-04 boundary changes hierarchy through bounded reveal/dissolve, color handoff, and one optional flash; no variable-speed time jump or arbitrary cut.

Density contract: Acts intentionally move sparse → stable → fractured → suspended → dense/released. Maximum intended simultaneous load is eight layers near frame 1605 and seven near frame 2655; this is a stress target, not a measured pass.

Temporal safety: Absolute-time speed/rate settings stay fixed. No graph output targets animation speed, camera cadence, shader travel/rotation speed, light motion speed, or particle integration rate unless a future consumer owns phase-continuous integration.

Palette: **void charcoal** `#05060B` (negative space); **ultraviolet** `#6D4AFF` (architecture); **electric cyan** `#32E6FF` (signal clarity); **signal magenta** `#FF3FB4` (impact and contrast); **human sodium amber** `#FF9E3D` (performer warmth); **peak white** `#F4F7FF` (strictly bounded accents).

## Breadth and evidence exclusions

Included families: `model-backed`, `procedural-spatial-3d`, `shader`, `particle-instancing`, `complementary-2d`, `camera`, `graph`, `compositor`. Curve Spectrum supplies complementary 2D language rather than excluding the entire category.

## Alternatives and risks

- `alternative-stage-only` — **rejected**: Preserves model quality but repeats Afterlight and cannot prove multi-layer/compositor breadth.
- `alternative-procedural-monolith` — **rejected**: Could look coherent but repeats Signal Cathedral and creates production-local component leakage.
- `alternative-shared-three-world` — **rejected**: Falsely assumes existing independent Three programs share a camera, depth, and occlusion contract.
- `alternative-human-signal` — **preferred-pending-gate-1**: Uses existing reusable breadth while keeping human performers primary and gaps small and canonical.

- `risk-capability-sampler`: Human Stage remains primary; every secondary layer has a unique act role and explicit review contribution.
- `risk-overdraw-and-independent-three-cost`: Bound active ranges, name stress frames, measure before approval, and remove redundant contribution rather than lower quality silently.
- `risk-temporal-jumps`: Keep absolute-time rates fixed and reject raw variable-rate graph bindings.
- `risk-white-clipping`: Disable internal Stage strobes/blinders and confine peak white to exact short Manual strobe ranges.
- `risk-model-material`: Retain male-cheer missing-normal-map warning as a visual review target; do not claim clean asset provenance.
- `risk-host-parity`: Use existing full-fidelity browser-bundle evidence until GAP-03 closes; never claim current local CLI renders advanced Three layers.

Forbidden leakage:

- No flagship frame numbers or act names inside reusable components or runtime evaluators.
- No production-only React store, transport field, hidden compositor branch, copied registry, or CLI special case.
- No generic model/facial/speech/root-motion system without a named criterion and authorized asset need.
- No automated wording that converts proposed aesthetic judgment into approval.

## Gate 1 human boundary

Automated checks can prove identities, ranges, references, types, host limitations, coverage, and document derivation. They cannot approve musical truth or creative quality. Gate 1 must judge:

- Is this the intended track and exact window?
- Does the five-act emotional arc feel musically true?
- Is Human Signal distinct and aesthetically coherent rather than a capability sampler?
- Are palette, material language, fixed Panoramic Sweep, and layer hierarchy right?
- Are all four performers worth retaining and visually grounded?
- Is the raster/image/video exclusion acceptable?
- Are GAP-01 through GAP-03 worth implementing as defined?

No automated check approves the treatment. Gate 1 must explicitly approve or correct it before aesthetic implementation.
