## Why The Final Vision Is Not The Literal Goal

The final vision intentionally includes open-ended future horizons:

- Viz Cloud
- Magnify production integration
- collaboration and external session hosting
- licensing and commercial boundaries
- specialized runners
- broader render backends and platforms
- future model, character, facial, speech, and singing systems

Those directions do not share one natural stopping condition, and several
require explicit product decisions. Telling an agent to “finish the vision”
would eventually force it to choose speculative product direction merely to
keep moving.

Goal Five therefore uses the complete vision as its decision filter while
giving autonomous work one enormous, concrete, verifiable terminal proof.

## Goal

Prove the local creative-production core of the VizEngine compounding vision
end to end by autonomously creating, refining, rendering, and certifying one
ambitious full-spectrum music-reactive flagship production while resolving
each confirmed reusable blocker to a mandatory completion criterion through
the clean shared engine, capability, authoring, inspection, or feedback
systems.

The result must prove VizEngine as one professional creative system:

- a human and an agent operate the same scene truth
- the preserved editor remains the serious live creative cockpit
- the project is genuinely multi-act, multi-layer, multi-component, and
  multi-graph
- model-backed 3D and authored animation remain first-class
- procedural, shader/GPU, particle/instanced, and complementary 2D or media
  systems can participate coherently
- musical direction exists at macro, phrase, and detail timescales
- preview, seek, inspection, stills, video, and portable reopen share one
  deterministic meaning
- the exact flagship remains responsive and performant in the real editor
- every engine change is reusable and no production-specific exception leaks
  into core
- the final video and fully editable project both meet an explicit quality bar

This is not a demo-production goal with some incidental engine work.

It is a creative-system maturation program whose terminal evidence is one
professional flagship production.

## Single Stopping Condition

Goal Five uses two immutable identities because a Git commit cannot contain its
own eventual hash:

1. Candidate commit `C` contains the exact implementation, project, capability,
   assets/artifact identities, and tests that pass every behavioral gate.
2. Evidence commit `E` is documentation-only and records `C`, its artifacts,
   validation, measurements, and human approvals.

Goal Five is complete at `E` only when the certification record shows every
mandatory production, canonical workflow, determinism, realtime performance,
parity, architecture, portability, documentation, and human creative-approval
criterion as passed for `C`. Only documentation validation is required on `E`.

Until then, continue checkpoint by checkpoint or pause at a declared human
gate or genuine documented blocker.

## Flagship Production Envelope

### Duration and musical structure

The selected music window must be between 45 and 60 seconds unless Gate 1
approves a 36–45 second window because it provides a materially stronger
musical structure.

The production must contain:

- four to six perceptibly distinct musical and visual acts
- at least three authored transitions
- one restrained or lower-density passage
- one clear build or escalation
- one primary high-energy statement
- one ending, release, transformation, or resolved final state

An act counts only when it is anchored to a documented musical section and
contains an approved perceptible change in composition, density, camera
posture, capability emphasis, or spatial hierarchy. Renamed intervals, metadata
labels, or palette-only changes do not count by themselves.

The project must contain an explicit deterministic musical map covering:

- section and phrase boundaries
- significant rhythmic or onset events
- longer energy envelopes
- contrast and release points
- camera and composition cues where applicable

### Three timescales of reactivity

Reactivity must be intentionally authored at three timescales:

1. Macro direction controls acts, density, composition, camera posture, palette
   families, and major entrances or removals.
2. Phrase direction controls transitions, sweeps, reveals, spatial evolution,
   and medium-duration envelopes.
3. Detail direction controls bounded pulses, lighting accents, particles,
   shader emphasis, movement nuance, and other short-lived response.

Every major graph output must have a named musical or directorial intention.
Arbitrary parameter vibration does not satisfy this goal.

Amplitude, intensity, rate, and phase remain distinct semantics. Variable rate
must be integrated into phase or consumed through an explicit phase-capable
contract; raw graph features may not modulate absolute-time speed in a way that
creates deterministic discontinuity.

### Honest creative breadth

The approved final treatment should normally contain:

- approximately 8–12 meaningful enabled visual layers
- at least four distinct visual component families
- several simultaneously visible layers at peak passages
- multiple graphs separated by creative responsibility
- native model-backed 3D with authored animation
- a procedural or spatial 3D system
- a shader-driven visual or effect system
- a bounded particle or instanced system
- a complementary 2D, SVG, image, video, graphic, or typography contribution
- meaningful compositor ordering, opacity, transforms, and blend behavior
- directed camera behavior
- shared global musical direction and component-local modulation
- both existing reusable capabilities and newly justified capabilities

These counts are breadth guards, not aesthetic targets. A medium may be
excluded when forcing it would weaken the production, but the treatment must
explain the exclusion and Gate 1 must approve it.

Every counted layer, component family, and graph must have:

- a named nonredundant creative responsibility
- a declared active interval
- a visible or inspectable contribution in review evidence
- at least one meaningful output or consumer where applicable

Disabled, effectively invisible, duplicate, count-only, or unused structures do
not count toward the production envelope.

The goal may not hide the full production inside one giant custom component.
The visible project structure must honestly express the composition.

### Creative coherence

The production must feel like one directed work rather than a capability demo
reel.

It must establish:

- an explicit emotional and energy arc
- a coherent palette and material language
- foreground, middle-distance, and background hierarchy where applicable
- motivated camera movement and transitions
- controlled density and negative space
- escalation without destroying legibility
- a recognizable visual identity distinct from Signal Cathedral and
  Afterlight Assembly

The aesthetic is deliberately not preselected in this document. It must emerge
from the authorized music and capability audit, then pass human treatment
approval.

## Production Treatment

Before committing to an aesthetic direction, the agent must audit:

- all authorized repository music and promising source windows
- models, clips, textures, images, video, shaders, and derived assets
- all component and node capabilities, presets, inputs, outputs, and renderer
  requirements
- current compositor, temporal, graph, model, render, and bundle semantics
- Signal Cathedral and Afterlight Assembly, including their friction reports
- current inspection, comparison, performance, and agent feedback tools

It must then write a bounded production treatment containing:

- selected track and exact contiguous window
- source and derivation provenance
- phrase, section, energy, and event analysis
- four-to-six-act creative arc
- visual hierarchy, palette, material, and camera language
- proposed layer, component, graph, asset, and artifact roles
- planned representative frame timestamps, motion windows, and optional
  aesthetic references
- the smallest justified reusable capability gaps
- performance and output targets
- risks, exclusions, and explicit non-goals

Gate 1 approves this treatment before deep aesthetic implementation.

## Architectural Workstreams

These workstreams are all subordinate to the one flagship objective. They are
not permission to build unrelated frameworks.

### 1. Production ownership and package topology

Gate 1 must establish one explicit ownership map before implementation:

- production project factory and production-specific graphs
- trusted production capability source, if required
- source and derived assets
- baked artifacts and manifests
- focused tests and fixtures
- Studio capability composition and sample registration
- evidence and generated media locations

The production should normally have one focused production package or equally
clear composition root. Production behavior may not scatter across core
components, example-project terrain, renderer packages, and Studio glue.

Dependency direction must remain explicit: production packages consume public
engine capabilities; generic engine packages never import or recognize the
flagship.

If Goal Five changes `VizProjectDocument` or another portable schema, it must:

- make one intentional schema-version decision
- update validation, actions, runtime, inspection, persistence, bundles, and
  repository-owned projects atomically
- remove superseded field ownership after migration
- avoid a compatibility reader, dual field model, or long-lived bridge unless
  separately approved with a deletion condition

### 2. Audio analysis and bake

The selected music window must prove the complete canonical audio path rather
than assuming it from earlier productions:

- source and derivative provenance
- explicit PCM decode ownership
- deterministic feature analysis and algorithm identity
- typed, versioned bake artifact identity
- runtime sampling at declared frames
- agreement between preview, direct seek, still, video, and portable reopen
- structured decode, bake, validation, and missing-artifact diagnostics
- bounded analysis performance, progress, cancellation, and resource cleanup

Existing machinery may satisfy the workstream without new architecture. The
goal must audit and certify it against the longer flagship before deciding that
new audio features or artifacts are needed.

Graphs remain responsible for artistic shaping. Baking supplies deterministic
musical evidence rather than hiding production direction.

### 3. Macro temporal direction

The flagship may not encode its acts as scattered magic timestamps inside
component implementations.

If the current project and graph contracts cannot express the approved score
cleanly, implement the smallest portable direction contract justified by the
production. It may include:

- named sections or cues with stable identities and frame-exact ranges
- deterministic section progress and cue-relative time
- inspectable graph inputs derived from the direction data
- canonical human and agent authoring operations
- direct-seek, sequential-playback, and render equivalence

This remains a lightweight direction layer over the canonical project timeline.
It must not become a second transport, hidden sequencer, speculative nonlinear
timeline, or component-private cue system.

### 4. Compositor-owned modulation

Act transitions may require graph-driven layer-level composition rather than
component settings pretending to own final scene composition.

If proven necessary, establish the smallest explicit compositor modulation
contract for:

- layer opacity
- layer transforms
- resolved composition values in runtime inspection
- visibility or another discrete property only if deterministic semantics are
  unambiguous
- existing alpha and blend behavior without regression

The compositor remains the owner of final ordering and combination. General
masks, arbitrary effect graphs, and a complete post-processing framework remain
deferred unless the approved production cannot achieve its intended result
without one specific reusable slice.

### 5. Multi-graph scale and inspection

The flagship must prove several canonical graphs operating together without a
second evaluator or graph dialect.

Graphs should separate responsibilities such as:

- global music direction
- acts and transitions
- camera intent
- performer or Stage response
- atmosphere and particles
- lighting, shaders, or effects
- graphic or media treatment

Exact count emerges from the composition. Acceptance requires:

- readable names and descriptions
- organized node layouts
- named outputs with clear consumers
- live values during playback
- graph-to-layer and output-usage inspection
- unused, missing, invalid, and unresolvable-binding diagnostics
- deterministic state under seek and render
- runtime cost attribution where the canonical evaluator can report it
  truthfully
- focused evaluation of unused work only where semantically safe

Subgraphs, templates, graph-to-graph execution, or a module system require real
duplication evidence and a stable small contract. They are not checklist
features.

### 6. Native models and asset-backed quality

The approved treatment must use the existing model and deterministic animation
substrate honestly.

Goal Five should expose the smallest reusable product surface needed outside a
Stage-only setting blob. A likely direction is a generic `Model3D` component
over the existing lower-level facilities with:

- deterministic clip selection, time, phase, or rate semantics
- transforms and direct seeking
- readiness and failure inspection
- hierarchy, material, clip, and morph inspection where the source provides it
- one real non-Stage consumer
- Stage remaining a specialized system over the same model substrate

This is evidence-sensitive rather than mandatory architecture. If the flagship
can use model-backed content cleanly through an already-canonical reusable
surface, do not build `Model3D` merely to satisfy a roadmap item.

If one preparation route becomes genuinely necessary, implement only that
real deterministic derivation and identity path. Do not build universal format
support.

Root motion, retargeting, facial performance, speech, singing, and general rig
inference remain outside Goal Five.

### 7. Reusable visual capability palette

When the approved creative idea exceeds the current component catalog, add the
smallest focused trusted capability needed by the production.

Every reusable visual capability must have:

- stable identity and implementation version
- typed data-only authoring schema
- useful bounds, defaults, descriptions, and presets
- explicit renderer and compatibility requirements
- deterministic runtime behavior
- inspectable inputs and resolved values
- complete resource readiness and disposal behavior
- focused correctness and lifecycle tests
- exact flagship performance measurements
- an independent fixture, second real consumer, or clearly stable domain
  boundary
- concise documentation and example usage

Production-specific palette, choreography, geometry arrangement, camera path,
asset selection, and timing remain project or production-pack content.

### 8. Agent authoring, perception, and feedback

The agent must create the flagship through canonical contracts rather than UI
imitation while the editor remains the optional live human head.

Goal Five may improve reusable tooling for:

- component, node, graph, asset, model, artifact, and renderer discovery
- revision-safe bulk transactions and semantic diffs
- direction/cue and compositor-binding operations
- frame-specific evaluated values and issue inspection
- graph/output usage and execution identity
- exact render requests
- named checkpoints and recovery
- section-aware review packets containing stills, contact sheets, motion clips,
  probes, diagnostics, and performance summaries
- source-mode and built-package parity
- live editor visibility without focus theft

Automated tools may detect blank, frozen, clipped, silent, missing-resource,
timing, determinism, or pacing problems. They may not invent a fake aesthetic
quality score.

### 9. Preserved editor product support

Every new portable concept must have an understandable editor surface. JSON or
CLI support alone does not complete a creator-facing capability.

The exact flagship must preserve:

- V1 visual language, information density, typography, layout, and control
  posture
- immediate visual feedback during continuous editing
- zero durable revisions during a gesture and exactly one on release
- animated graphs and runtime debug values without React as the signal bus
- transport, waveform, history, undo/redo, persistence, import/export, Jobs,
  Rhythm Lab, diagnostics, profiler, and render workflows
- human and agent activity in the same canonical history
- keyboard and focus safety for touched workflows
- clean ordinary-use console and runtime diagnostics

### 10. Exact flagship performance and lifecycle

Performance must be certified on the full approved flagship, not inferred from
Light Tunnel or a small isolated fixture.

Activation must pin the exact environment, viewport, quality profile, warm-up,
observation window, output cadence, and budgets before optimization.

Required measurements include:

- graph-closed and graph-open playback
- display cadence and long-frame distribution
- runtime planning, graph, layer, compositor, and renderer costs where
  truthfully measurable
- act-boundary and seek spikes
- pointer-to-transient and pointer-to-visible latency under load
- zero movement revisions and one release commit
- repeated playback, editing, model loading, rendering, project reopen, and
  disposal cycles
- browser heap and renderer resource steady state
- final 1080p render throughput and media correctness
- unchanged Light Tunnel certification

Gate 0 freezes the machine, browser, harness, measurement method, minimum
absolute budgets, and baseline Light Tunnel profile. Gate 3 freezes the exact
flagship candidate revision, enabled content, viewport, DPR, backing resolution,
quality multiplier, act windows, and observation windows used for full-project
certification.

A lower-resolution authoring profile may exist only as a secondary explicit
profile with visual-comparison evidence. It may not become the sole profile
used to claim flagship performance. Hidden layer removal or quality reduction
is not performance success.

## Reusable Promotion Rule

Use this incubation sequence:

1. Production score, palette, choreography, asset choice, layer composition,
   graph instances, and timing values begin as project content.
2. New parameterized executable visual behavior may begin in a trusted
   production-local capability pack.
3. Behavior moves to a broader reusable pack only after two meaningfully
   different consumers prove it. A stable-domain-boundary exception must cite
   an existing governing contract and include a non-flagship fixture exercising
   a materially different configuration; architectural preference alone is not
   evidence.
4. Engine core changes only for scene-independent infrastructure belonging to
   canonical project, session, runtime, compositor, asset, bake, render, or
   control semantics.
5. Agent tools wrap the same contracts used by the editor and CLI.

The goal should leave behind, when honestly justified:

- at least one reusable visual capability or substantial extension
- at least one reusable node, temporal, graph, compositor, model, or renderer
  capability
- at least one authoring, inspection, comparison, or feedback improvement

If any category would be artificial, record that finding rather than inventing
an abstraction to meet a count.

Review the exact candidate final video and reopened editable project.

changes are ordinary Goal Five work rather than goal failure.

documentation, inspection tooling, and evidence work. They may not continue
expensive aesthetic divergence beyond the gate.

## Multi-Agent Operating Model

Subagents are bounded specialists, not competing owners.

### Lead and integrator

The lead owns:

- goal state and checkpoint selection
- architecture and product judgment
- integration diff and commits
- human-gate requests
- evidence quality and completion claims

Only the lead updates goal status or certifies a checkpoint.

### Architecture investigator

Read-only by default. It maps:

- canonical and forbidden owners
- dependencies and contracts
- likely deletion and simplification
- duplicate truth or production-specific leakage
- focused test and migration surface

### Product and parity verifier

It validates:

- preserved editor behavior
- live interaction and history
- affected V1 workflows
- browser diagnostics
- persistence and portability

It reports evidence rather than implementation claims.

### Creative and performance verifier

It inspects:

- stills, contact sheets, and motion clips
- musical intent, pacing, camera, transitions, and hierarchy
- frame pacing, interaction latency, and resource lifecycle
- media probes and final-output defects

Implementation may be delegated only when package and file ownership is
explicitly disjoint. No two agents edit the same paths concurrently. The lead
reviews all contributions and reruns validation; a subagent report alone is not
certification evidence.

## Checkpoint Loop

Every checkpoint follows this loop:

1. Recover canonical context, resume marker, evidence, Git state, and running
   product truth.
2. Select the smallest high-leverage unfinished flagship-blocking criterion.
3. Name the canonical owner, forbidden duplicate owners, visible result,
   deletion target, and acceptance before editing.
4. Reproduce or measure the need before abstracting or optimizing.
5. Use bounded read-only specialist agents where parallel investigation helps.
6. Implement one primary conceptual axis through the smallest reusable
   architecture justified by evidence.
7. Exercise the change immediately in the real flagship.
8. Run focused validation and inspect real output.
9. Review the complete diff for dead code, duplicate truth, accidental
   configurability, debug artifacts, and simplification.
10. Update the resume marker, checkpoint evidence, current state when material,
    the ledger for milestones, and suggestions for non-active durable work.
11. Commit one coherent validated checkpoint when authorized and safe.
12. Leave the worktree clean, stop all servers/browsers/encoders/children, and
    name exactly one next checkpoint or human gate.

```text
Measurements:

```

Detailed measurements belong in evidence artifacts. The ledger records only
meaningful milestones. Suggestions contain durable opportunities outside the
active goal, not a second active backlog.

The Phase 0 criteria matrix is stricter than checkpoint prose. Every mandatory
row must include:

- a stable criterion ID and exact requirement
- the command, browser journey, render workflow, or human review that evaluates
  it
- the pinned machine, browser, viewport, DPR, quality, execution mode, and
  source revision that matter to the result
- exact frames, times, act boundaries, gestures, cycles, or observation window
- an equality rule, numeric budget, perceptual tolerance, or explicitly human
  decision rule
- durable raw and summarized artifact locations
- the evaluator responsible for the decision

A criterion without an executable or reviewable decision rule is incomplete,
not pending implementation.

## Validation Tiers

### Tier 0: Every-run preflight

- branch, worktree, status, diff, and log inspection
- resume-marker and actual-commit reconciliation
- active checkpoint and affected parity identification
- confirmation that no competing mutation loop exists
- confirmation that the previous certified baseline is recoverable

### Tier 1: Inner loop

- focused package types
- focused unit and regression tests
- project and schema validation
- targeted deterministic frame or graph evaluation
- `git diff --check`

### Tier 2: Checkpoint acceptance

- affected package tests and builds
- bundle validate/reopen when affected
- representative still or contact-sheet output
- relevant failure-path tests
- affected browser workflow in headless Chromium
- console and runtime issue inspection
- complete diff review and cleanup
- evidence record update

### Tier 3: Integration milestone

Run when an act, reusable capability family, or canonical workflow becomes
complete:

- full flagship project validation
- editor open, edit, save, and reopen
- headless agent mutation plus live editor observation
- direct-seek and sequential-frame equivalence
- representative multi-act motion clip
- affected performance benchmark
- resource creation and disposal checks
- `pnpm check:foundation`

### Tier 4: Creative calibration

- deliberately selected stills
- contact sheet covering every act and transition
- motion proxy with intended audio
- human review and explicit decision

### Tier 5: Final certification

- one uninterrupted green `pnpm check:foundation`
- all Goal Five production suites
- fixed-device realtime benchmark
- graph-open interaction and seek benchmarks
- endurance and renderer-resource lifecycle
- deterministic random-access and repeated-render evidence
- portable bundle roundtrip from a clean environment
- final 1080p video and media probe
- final human creative approval
- exact clean candidate commit `C` plus documentation-only evidence commit `E`

An isolated rerun helps diagnose a flaky aggregate test. It is not a final
certification pass.

## Default Performance Budgets

Phase 0 must freeze exact budgets from a clean activation baseline under Gate
0's approved performance envelope. Recommended defaults for the Apple M1 Pro /
Chromium target are:

- at least 58 displayed FPS after warm-up on the approved full flagship live
  profile
- display-interval p95 no worse than 20 ms
- no recurring stall above 100 ms
- graph-open playback within 10% of graph-closed performance
- continuous-control pointer-to-visible p95 no worse than 32 ms
- immediate pointer-to-transient publication consistent with existing
  sub-millisecond-class evidence
- zero durable revisions during movement and exactly one on release
- six-cycle endurance with no monotonic renderer-resource growth
- forced-GC heap growth below a frozen budget, initially recommended at 16 MB
- unchanged certified Light Tunnel planning and playback budgets

These are starting thresholds, not permission to relax existing stronger
metrics without evidence. Final thresholds may become stricter after the
representative prototype. They may not be weakened merely to pass the final
production.

## Determinism, Portability, and Media Protocol

Phase 0 must convert these rules into executable criteria rather than relying
on the word "equivalent":

- semantic evaluation compares the same project identity, revision, execution
  manifest, frame/time, cue and active-layer state, graph outputs, resolved
  parameters, resource readiness, and runtime issues; discrete values are
  exact and numeric values use field-specific frozen tolerances
- sequential, cold, direct-seek, checkpoint-replay, preview, still, video, and
  adapter paths are compared only after their initialization and sampling
  contracts are made explicit
- decoded video frames use pinned exact or perceptual image tolerances by
  criterion; encoded H.264 bytes are not presumed identical unless the frozen
  encoder contract explicitly guarantees that
- decoded audio has the declared stream, sample rate, channel layout, start
  time, duration, and synchronization tolerance; intentional silence is
  declared by treatment interval rather than inferred after a failure
- portable reopen starts from a fresh browser profile and empty task-specific
  cache/artifact roots, with network disabled, the candidate build from `C`,
  and only bundle-contained assets plus approved built-in capability packs; it
  may not resolve source-workstation absolute paths or undeclared caches
- automated black, clipped-white, freeze, discontinuity, and silence detectors
  flag review intervals; an interval passes only when it is absent or its
  intentional use and bounds were declared in the approved treatment

The exact comparison fields, tools, tolerances, and artifacts are frozen at
Gate 0 and may be changed later only to correct a demonstrated invalid test.
Such a change requires an evidence note and rerun of every affected criterion.

## Operationally Quiet Autonomous Runs

The persistent `/goal` loop is the only mutation owner by default. Scheduled
workers remain read-only auditors while it is active. A scheduled worker may
mutate only after the persistent loop is disabled or after a manually proven
handoff system provides all of the following:

- one atomic shared lease with expiry and recovery semantics
- one persistent integration branch and exact starting commit
- explicit commit, integration, conflict, and branch-advance policy
- a dry run proving that two workers cannot acquire mutation authority
- a reviewed first mutating run before recurring unattended use

An isolated worktree is not a writer lock. A scheduler observing another
owner, an awaiting-human checkpoint, or a stale integration base performs
read-only validation or exits without changes.

Unattended or scheduled checkpoints must:

- avoid visible Chrome, DevTools, Finder, editor, or other focus-stealing
  windows
- never play system or browser audio
- use headless muted Chromium when browser proof is necessary
- use one heavy process at a time
- keep Playwright single-worker
- avoid concurrent builds, browser suites, and renders
- prefer stills and contact sheets for routine visual feedback
- use short proxy clips only when motion must be inspected
- reserve full-resolution full-duration renders for declared milestones
- avoid headed fixed-device performance checks without human coordination
- stop every server, browser, encoder, and child process at checkpoint end
- switch to a light diagnostic, test, cleanup, or documentation checkpoint when
  the machine is already heavily loaded

An isolated worktree does not isolate CPU, RAM, GPU, disk, fans, audio, or
visible applications.

Browser work is allowed unattended only after a dedicated automation profile
has been manually proven with a unique port, `reuseExistingServer: false`,
headless mode, muted audio, isolated task-specific artifacts, and reliable
cleanup. Until then, scheduled work must avoid browser execution. Headed
fixed-device performance certification always remains a coordinated human-time
checkpoint.

Before recurrence is enabled, run one scheduled prompt manually, inspect its
diff and process cleanup, and review its validation artifacts. Keep network
mutation disabled by default, use the narrowest available filesystem scope,
expire stale worktrees and artifacts by a documented policy, and never enable
push, merge, publication, deployment, or external-system mutation.

## Anti-Rabbit-Hole Rules

- One checkpoint has one primary conceptual axis.
- Do not combine a major refactor and an unrelated major feature.
- Diagnose before optimizing or abstracting.
- If 30–40 minutes of investigation does not narrow the cause, record
  competing hypotheses and stop that avenue.
- After two failed implementation strategies, return to the ownership model
  and redefine the slice.
- If two consecutive checkpoints show no visible, measurable, or structural
  flagship advancement, perform an integration and direction audit.
- A reusable abstraction needs two plausible consumers or one clearly stable
  domain boundary.
- A temporary bridge needs an owner and near-term deletion condition.
- Do not build a new capability until the current catalog has been inspected
  and shown insufficient.
- Profile before changing performance ownership or adding memoization.
- If a narrow checkpoint unexpectedly crosses several architecture boundaries,
  stop and rescope.
- Reserve the final portion of each run for validation, evidence, cleanup, and
  process shutdown.
- Every four to six checkpoints, perform an integration, deletion, and
  documentation pass before expanding further.

## Forbidden Shortcuts

Goal Five must not introduce:

- project, layer, graph, or asset identity checks in generic engine code
- production timestamps inside reusable component implementations
- a flagship-only runtime, renderer, graph evaluator, document, transport,
  audio, history, bundle, bake, or export path
- an agent shadow project or private mutation language
- pre-rendered footage standing in for an editable live capability
- component-private final compositing
- React or Zustand as frame-rate, graph-signal, meter, or pointer-rate truth
- broad memoization as the primary performance architecture
- warning suppression, test weakening, threshold loosening, or favorable
  fixture replacement merely to obtain green output
- arbitrary executable code embedded in project bundles
- a giant configurable abstraction built ahead of demonstrated need
- visible quality, layer, graph, or feature reduction to pass performance
- technically valid final media without the editable portable project
- deep polish of one act before the complete macro structure exists
- overlapping mutation agents against the same worktree

## Explicit Deferrals

Unless measured flagship evidence proves a direct blocker and the human
explicitly expands scope, Goal Five excludes:

- Viz Cloud, auth, billing, collaboration, and hosted execution
- Magnify production integration
- licensing and premium product decisions
- untrusted plugin loading and marketplaces
- multi-user external session hosting
- native or browser-free video execution
- WebGPU migration or universal renderer abstraction
- universal model import and preparation
- root motion, retargeting, facial, speech, and singing systems
- generalized masks, arbitrary effect graphs, and a full nonlinear timeline
- generalized subgraph, template, or graph-module frameworks
- archive-grade large-media exchange
- universal cross-browser, device, accessibility, or deployed-scale
  certification
- exact cross-driver pixel identity
- broad dependency modernization or another line-count program

These remain legitimate final-vision directions rather than hidden Goal Five
backlog.

## Failure And Stop Rules

Continue autonomously through ordinary code, test, build, browser, render,
visual, performance, and first-architecture-attempt failures.

Use these recovery rules:

- flaky test: preserve trace, rerun once in isolation for diagnosis, then fix
  the source or harness before certification
- performance regression: preserve measurement, identify ownership, and
  restore the budget without silently lowering quality or thresholds
- nondeterminism: isolate the earliest divergent frame and state before adding
  more production complexity
- missing resource: verify resolver and execution identity; never hardcode a
  workstation path
- environment failure: retry once, record versions and logs, then select
  useful light work if available
- interrupted run: preserve artifacts and diff, kill child processes, and
  leave an exact resume marker

Stop and request human direction for:

- product-vision or material UX changes
- accepted feature, quality, or performance degradation
- a major scene-language or architecture paradigm choice
- large dependency or infrastructure commitment
- licensing, publication, deployment, billing, cloud, or external-system action
- a major quality-versus-performance trade
- a declared creative calibration
- ambiguous user work that cannot be safely isolated

When human input is required, mark the checkpoint `awaiting-human`, present no
more than three concrete options with tradeoffs, yield without falsely marking
the goal complete or blocked, and stop only the affected decision path. A
scheduled worker treats `awaiting-human` as read-only and should be disabled in
the scheduler UI until the decision is recorded.

- honest multi-layer, multi-component, and multi-graph structure

  undo, redo, save, close, and reopen

- live values animate correctly
- interaction remains immediate and commit semantics remain correct
- project structure is understandable without source archaeology
- ordinary-use console and runtime diagnostics are clean

### Performance and lifecycle

- frozen full-flagship live, graph-open, seek, interaction, and endurance
  budgets pass without disabled content or unapproved quality reduction
- renderer resources return to steady state
- no progressive cadence or memory degradation
- every new retained program has bounded allocation and disposal evidence
- Light Tunnel performance remains certified

### Reusable system improvement

- every promoted capability names its consumers and governing package/spec
- production-only composition remains outside generic engine core
- no duplicate truth or long-lived transitional path remains
- new authoring and agent surfaces converge on canonical operations
- independent fixtures or consumers prove reusable behavior

## Scheduled-Run Prompt

Once Goal Five is active, the primary mutation loop has yielded, and the
manually proven sole-writer handoff requirements are satisfied, an isolated
checkpoint worker may use:

```text
Advance the active VizEngine Goal Five program by exactly one bounded,
high-leverage checkpoint. Recover from AGENTS.md, the compounding vision,
current state, the active goal, the autonomous compass, governing specs,
affected parity rows, latest evidence, and actual Git state. Work only from the
latest integrated Goal Five checkpoint and do not compete with another mutation
loop.

Use one primary change axis; preserve canonical project/session/runtime and V1
product quality; reject production-specific engine exceptions. Validate the
real product, review and simplify the complete diff, update the resume marker
and evidence. Create one coherent local checkpoint commit only when the result
is a complete validated Tier 2 checkpoint; otherwise leave no commit, preserve
diagnostic evidence, and report the incomplete finding. Leave the worktree
clean unless a declared recovery record intentionally preserves a diff, and
stop all child processes.

Remain operationally quiet: no visible applications, focus theft, or audio;
headless muted Chromium only; one heavy process at a time; no routine
full-resolution video. Do not push, merge, publish, deploy, or change external
```

The persistent `/goal` is the primary multi-day development engine. Scheduled
isolated runs are read-only by default and become supplementary checkpoint
writers only when commit continuity and atomic sole-writer discipline are
