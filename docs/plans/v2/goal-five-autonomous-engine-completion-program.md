## Outcome

Complete the local VizEngine product horizon defined by the compounding vision:

- a deterministic, portable, high-performance visual runtime
- a polished professional editor preserving the V1 capability and interaction
  floor
- one shared live session controlled equally by people, agents, the product CLI,
  and embedding hosts
- excellent model-backed, procedural 3D, shader, particle, instancing, 2D,
  compositor, camera, audio-reactive, and graph-driven rendering
- reliable file, asset, artifact, bake, bundle, render, and export workflows
- reusable visualization components and graph tools proven through real creative
  use
- one ambitious, polished, multi-act, multilayer, perfectly music-reactive
  flagship video and its complete editable project
- deterministic inspection, visual, motion, audio, performance, lifecycle,
  portability, and code-quality feedback sufficient for autonomous dogfooding

The program ends only when the engine horizon is supported by a clean candidate,
full product evidence, final media, and a handoff packet. It does not end merely
because Human Signal renders once.

## Delegated Authority

The product owner delegates all reversible local decisions that remain inside
the documented engine vision. The agent owns:

- creative direction and refinement within the approved Human Signal treatment
- architecture, refactoring, cleanup, deletion, and schema evolution
- editor UX parity, performance, responsiveness, organization, and polish
- component, node, graph, renderer, compositor, model, animation, asset, bake,
  file, export, and inspection capabilities
- reusable visual-capability and visualization-tool growth driven by real
  production needs
- local dependencies and implementation languages when evidence justifies them
- headless authoring, same-session live editor updates, agent feedback, and
  deterministic production tooling
- tests, benchmarks, browser checks, media analysis, visual review, evidence,
  commits, and same-branch fast-forward pushes

This authority includes a Rust or WebAssembly implementation when profiling and
ownership analysis show that it is the clean final architecture. It does not
authorize a speculative parallel engine, novelty rewrite, or technology choice
without a measured need.

## Human Stop Boundary

Autonomous work does not pause for reversible aesthetic choices, implementation
details, refactors, dependencies, renderer design, test strategy, or ordinary
uncertainty that can be resolved through repository evidence and bounded
experiments.

Stop and request human direction only when the next action would:

- start the separately governed hosted platform, cloud service, collaboration
  service, or Magnify production integration horizon
- define pricing, monetization, licensing policy, commercial packaging, or paid
  external-service commitments
- publish, deploy, release, merge to `main`/`master`, open or merge a pull
  request, purchase, or mutate production/external systems without separate
  authority
- materially change the compounding vision or intentionally accept a product,
  parity, quality, or performance regression
- overwrite ambiguous user work that cannot be isolated safely

When the agent reaches this boundary, it produces the completed engine handoff
first and asks one concrete next-horizon question. Human silence never authorizes
an external or commercial action.

## Autonomous Calibration

Former Gates 2 through 5 are independent calibration milestones under the
[typed delegated review contract](../../parity/goal-five-delegated-review.md).
Each calibration must combine:

- deterministic and structural validation
- representative still and motion inspection
- intended-audio review through silent machine analysis and generated media
  evidence during unattended runs
- independent visual, architecture, and acceptance critique
- explicit defect and uncertainty records
- another refinement slice whenever evidence does not support the quality claim

The agent may make and revise creative choices. It must not manufacture proof:
source presence is not visual quality, one favorable frame is not motion quality,
and a numeric image score is not creative coherence. Final acceptance is an
evidence-backed agent judgment under the product owner's delegated authority.

## Program Shape

The machine program at
`tools/repo/programs/goal-five-engine-completion.json` is the execution truth.
It contains one dependency graph from policy alignment through final handoff.
Each scheduled wake claims at most one item, creates at most one coherent commit,
and stops cleanly before another wake.

### Wave 1: delegated proof and whole-production skeleton

- reconcile human-only certification language with delegated review
- materialize the exact audio window, bake, project, production package, and
  low-detail five-act skeleton
- prove canonical headless authoring and same-session editor visibility
- build and evaluate one representative quality slice without losing the full
  production structure

### Wave 2: canonical direction and compositor semantics

- implement the smallest portable macro-direction contract
- implement graph-driven compositor modulation through canonical layer values
- carry schema, validation, actions, history, runtime, inspection, editor, CLI,
  bundle, and deterministic semantics together

  encodings

### Wave 3: renderer and capability breadth

- harden native models, grounding, animation, material, and lifecycle behavior
- integrate procedural 3D, shaders, particles, instancing, complementary 2D,
  camera, lighting, and compositing
- add reusable capabilities only at proven stable boundaries
- establish the profiling boundary and evaluate JavaScript, Three, workers,
  WebAssembly, Rust, or another local implementation without prematurely
  selecting a backend from an incomplete workload

### Wave 4: multi-graph, agent control, files, and exports

- scale responsibility-separated graphs and directorial signals
- complete agent-readable usage, cost, issue, comparison, and live-session tools
- prove editor and headless operations use the same actions and state
- complete asset/file management, portable bundles, deterministic renders, video
  exports, cancellation, progress, diagnostics, and clean resource disposal

### Wave 5: complete production and autonomous creative refinement

- finish every act, transition, entrance, exit, camera move, palette progression,
  density change, and three-timescale musical response
- repeatedly review exact stills and motion windows
- refine model grounding, animation, lighting, exposure, hierarchy, particles,
  continuity, and reactive clarity until the evidence supports a professional
  quality claim
- ensure the editor project remains understandable and fully editable

### Wave 6: performance, parity, and lifecycle hardening

- measure before optimizing under the exact full composition, then finalize the

- meet playback, graph-open interaction, seeking, transport, history, render,
  and endurance budgets without reducing content or quality
- preserve or improve the 42-row V1 experience and Light Tunnel floor
- fix bottlenecks only at their canonical owners and remove obsolete paths

### Wave 7: consolidation, candidate, and final evidence

- remove temporary scaffolding, duplicate truth, dead code, debug artifacts, and
  production-specific engine leakage
- certify previous productions, clean-room portability, deterministic multi-path
  equivalence, all browser workflows, builds, and complete repository checks
- freeze a clean candidate, generate final 1080p H.264/AAC media and the complete
  visual-review packet, and validate the editable project
- create the evidence-only terminal commit and engine-completion handoff

## Scheduling Contract

The product owner's 2026-09-06 instruction authorizes daily 01:00–08:00 in
`Europe/Ljubljana`, with scheduled opportunities every 15 minutes through 07:45.
Each wake claims at most one checkpoint; an active run may continue across later
opportunities, which exit without mutation while its lease is valid. Check time
at major boundaries, begin winding down by 07:30 and stop development by 08:00.
Preserve incomplete work through the runbook's exact recoverable block procedure
and release ownership; never discard work to meet the clock.

Runs remain headless and muted. They notify only for a checkpoint failure that
needs attention, completed engine horizon, external/monetization boundary, or
an unsafe state requiring the owner. Routine progress and healthy no-op wakes
remain quiet.

GPT-6 Astra is the preferred scheduled model because this program requires
long-horizon architecture, computer use, visual judgment, and sustained
instruction following. The app-owned schedule is the authority for the actual
model and cadence; repository text records intent but does not impersonate host
configuration.

## Terminal Definition

The engine horizon is complete when all of the following are true:

1. The exact Human Signal project and portable bundle are complete, valid,
   editable, and understandable in Studio.
2. The final video is full-duration 1920×1080 H.264/AAC with intended audio,
   correct synchronization, and no unexplained visual or audio defects.
3. All 46 Goal Five criteria pass through their current delegated or automated
   evidence contracts.
4. Full flagship playback, editing, seeking, rendering, and six-cycle lifecycle
   budgets pass without content reduction.
5. Every affected V1 parity workflow and previous certified production remains
   green.
6. Human and agent control share one session, action, history, runtime, file,
   asset, export, and inspection truth.
7. Reusable component and graph capabilities have real consumers and remain
   outside production-specific engine leakage.
8. The candidate diff is minimal, canonical, typed, documented, tested, and free
   of obsolete architecture.
9. A final engine handoff names the exact candidate, evidence, media, remaining
   non-engine horizon, and the next platform/monetization decision.

Anything less is progress, not completion.
